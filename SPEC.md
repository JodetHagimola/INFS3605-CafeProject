# INFS3605-CafeProject

## Who uses it
Small café owners like Margaret who manage stock and supplier orders themselves and need to reduce shortages, waste, and time spent on inventory checks.

## What they can do
- View current stock levels for each ingredient in one dashboard.
- Record stock received, used, or wasted and update inventory quantities.
- Identify low-stock ingredients that need replenishment.
- Forecast daily customer demand using historical sales and upcoming holidays.
- View recommended reorder quantities and timing based on current stock and expected demand.

## Out of scope for now
- Real-time supplier market price comparisons.
- Fully automated purchasing and payments to suppliers.
- Separate ordering and approval workflows for chefs.
- Automated invoice matching and supplier dispute management.
- Physical stock tracking through sensors, cameras, or robots.
